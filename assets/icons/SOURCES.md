`materialsymbolsrounded`, `materialsymbolssharp`), at 24 px, weight 400, grade 0: each icon
plain (`<name>_24px.xml`) and filled (`<name>_fill1_24px.xml`), at their original paths.
Downloaded 2026-10-30 (24,942 files).

`source/material-symbols/metadata.json` is Google's icon metadata (categories, search tags,
popularity), from https://fonts.google.com/metadata/icons, filtered to the icons in Material
Symbols.
