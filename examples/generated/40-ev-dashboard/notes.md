# EV dashboard: evaluation notes (run 2)

Final document: `dash.json` (generated by `gen.mjs`), converted to `dash.rc` (13,898 B).
Host variables: `speed` (km/h), `battery` (%), `range` (km), `turn` (0 straight, 1 left, 2 right, 3 slight left, 4 slight right, 5 U-turn, 6 roundabout), `distance` (m), and `street` (a host string).

## 1. Tool calls in order (20)

1. `list`: read the server overview and workflow (build_kit, then components, then convert/render/audit). I followed that workflow.
2. `build_kit` with the verbatim goal (kit.txt) returned Gauge and Icon, plus design rules: "Low is the alarm" (Gauge `bandLo: 20, bandHi: 100`) and "Turn arrows are icons" (turn_left etc., distance via step()). The examples (HST-07, EXP-04, CMP-08) were mostly irrelevant. I decided on a Gauge with a band for the battery, Material turn icons, and a big text for the speed.
3. `reference` query "animate host variable change ease…": found the animated value `{"value", "anim"}` and the host string syntax. It did not say where an animated value can be declared.
4. `reference` topic format: covered stateLayout, visibility rules, textFromFloat flags, the spacedBy+weight trap and semantics. I decided on a stateLayout keyed on `step(windowWidth(), windowHeight()*1.3)` for wide vs square.
5. `reference` query on animated variables: an animated local belongs in a component's `locals`. I decided to write inline components (SpeedReadout, BatteryPanel, NavPanel).
6. `reference` topic components: params/locals syntax.
7. `convert` check:true: it converted on the first try (13.7 KB) and listed the host variables.
8. `render` variants:true showed the dark, light and mono looks. Problems: the TextChoice text was tiny, "800 m" wrapped in the square layout, and the tiny size overflowed.
9. `get` TextChoice: it takes fontSize/color/maxLines. I styled it.
10. `render` samples ×7 at 800×480 gave component_overflow in the nav row, which was my use of spacedBy with a weighted child (a documented trap), and "1.2 km" was clipped. I restructured the nav panel vertically (icon / distance / direction / street).
11. `render` samples again: no findings. I checked every direction and the distances 0/50/1200/25000 plus a long street (my own contact sheet).
12. `audit`: 0 findings.
13. `describe inspect`: looked up the format of `interact` actions.
14. `inspect` interact (set speed 130, wait 0.3 s): the eased local was at 115.1 mid-way, which confirms speed and range ease.
15. `render` 600×600 light, 2 samples: good. Low battery at 8 % showed amber, not red, so I aligned the "Low battery" text threshold to 20 % (the band edge).
16. `inspect` accessibility: the speed has a label. The Gauge's only label is the static "Battery %" (no value), and the icon label duplicated the direction text. I added a live battery/range contentDescription and removed the icon labels.
17. `render` variants: all four looks were OK. A tiny size clipped the range text, and short 1280×288 visibly clipped the street at the card bottom **with no finding reported**.
18. `describe render`: the sizes/sheet options.
19. `render` sizes [1280×480, 800×480, 600×600, 1280×288, 640×288], sheet, worst-case values: everything fits.
20. `convert` check: final size 13,898 B.

## 2. Dead ends / wasted calls
- Call 10's overflow was my own mistake. The trap is documented, but only in prose deep in the format topic, and the component guide's examples don't warn about it. Calls 8 and 10 each needed a relayout. A layout lint at convert time ("spacedBy on a row with a weighted child") would have caught it before rendering.
- Calls 3 and 5 were two reference queries to find out where `anim` is allowed (in a component local, yes; in resources.variables, unknown). One sentence in the format topic would avoid this.
- Calls 13 and 18 (`describe`) were only needed because the list output doesn't give the formats of the sub-parameters (actions, sizes).
- So: 1 real dead end (the spacedBy row) and 4 partly wasted calls.

## 3. Wrong, misleading, noisy or missing
- The brief asks for "a drawn arrow, not a text glyph", and build_kit says "Turn arrows are icons". I took that at face value, so the arrows are Material vector icons, not a custom drawing. That is reasonable, but the roundabout code has no exit number: `roundabout_right` doesn't show *which* exit. The kit never mentions the exit number.
- Gauge zones: with a band, a critically low battery (8 %) is only **amber**. The kit's "Low is the alarm" rule says to use the band ("green above 20 %, amber below"), but there is no red/error tier for a critical value. I had to add a red text myself.
- Gauge accessibility: "label … also the accessibility label". The label is static ("Battery %"), so a screen reader never hears the value. The doc should say so.
- `inspect accessibility` with `variables: {"battery": 8}` still reported "312 km range", and listed the hidden "Low battery" text with bounds `w 3.17, h -5.14`. Either the variables were not applied, or the snapshot was taken mid-ease. Hidden (GONE) nodes should not appear, and negative sizes are noise.
- `render` variants at short 1280×288: the street text was cut off at the bottom of the card (visible in the image), but "findings: none". The text_overflow detection misses vertical clipping by a parent clip.
- The build_kit examples HST-07 (font-scale card) and EXP-04 (canvas remap) were labelled "the goal's subject: car, turn" / "speed, range". They were weak matches, and "ev" was unmatched.
- The `list` output is 30 KB, with the same tools repeated across 5 servers (build_kit and check_component appear twice).

## 4. What I would have liked
- A navigation/turn component (icon by code, plus distance formatting with "Now" / m / km) or an example of one. Everyone writes the same step() ladder.
- A Gauge `criticalAt` (red below N with a band) and a value-bearing accessibility label.
- A convert-time layout lint for spacedBy combined with weights.
- A note in reference/format on whether `{"value", "anim"}` works in resources.variables, or only in component locals.
- Overflow detection for text clipped vertically by a clipped parent.

## 5. Assessment of the final display
- **Correctness:** at every sample the speed, battery % on a banded arc, km range, and "Low battery" (below 20 %, red, with a battery_alert icon) are right. All 7 turn codes show the right Material icon (straight, turn_left/right, turn_slight_left/right, u_turn_left, roundabout_right) via a stateLayout on `@turn`, plus a TextChoice word. Distance: below 20 m "Now"; 20–949 m rounded to 10 m ("50 m", "800 m"); up to 9.9 km one decimal ("1.2 km"); above that whole km ("25 km"). The street is a host string: up to 2 lines, then an ellipsis.
- **Easing:** the speed, battery arc and range are animated locals (0.8 s), confirmed by interact (mid-ease 115 on the way from 88 to 130).
- **Sizes:** 1280×480 and 800×480 use the wide row (battery | speed | nav). 600×600 and 480×480 use the square layout (big speed on top, battery + nav below), chosen by stateLayout on the window aspect. The speed font scales with windowWidth. No findings at 1280×480, 800×480, 600×600, 1280×288 or 640×288.
- **Themes:** the theme colours follow light and dark. Mono-dark and mono-light render correctly; the nav card loses its fill in mono (no outline).
- **Accessibility:** document description; speed "Speed N kilometres per hour"; battery "Battery N percent, N kilometres range"; the turn word, distance and street are text nodes. The labels use the raw (non-eased) values.
- **Bytes:** 13.9 KB. Seven icons plus the two layout copies make up most of it.
- **Weak spots:**
  - No roundabout exit number.
  - Low battery is amber on the arc (red only in the text).
  - The range text sits far below the gauge in the wide layout: the 240° gauge leaves empty space.
  - Content is duplicated in two layouts (inline components keep the source small).
  - The nav card has no outline in mono.
  - The icon is reused for left/right roundabouts (driving side not configurable).
