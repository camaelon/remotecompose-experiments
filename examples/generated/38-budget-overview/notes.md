## 2. Dead ends / wasted calls

- Call 9's widget renders repeated a clipping I had not fixed properly (a weighted empty spacer box before a wrap-content status box still left the status wrapping). A layout explanation in the finding ("the box got N px for M px of text") would have pointed straight at the cause.
- Call 9 dropped the large-amount sample silently, which forced a re-render (call 10). If the cap had been documented or reported, I would have split the request up front.
- Call 6 (`describe render`) was only needed because `list` truncates the parameter docs. It was cheap, but still a call.
No real dead ends otherwise: 1 dead end counted (call 9, partly wasted).

## 3. Wrong, misleading, noisy or missing in tool output

- render `samples` says "up to 12", but with `sizes` the product is capped (5 samples x 2 sizes gave **8 renders**, sample 5 missing) with no message saying anything was dropped.
- The `variants` contact sheet (out/v1-variants-sheet.png) lays cells out overlapping: tall phone renders run into the next row, and the captions ("light theme", "mono-dark (always-on displays)") are printed on top of other images. It is hard to read; I had to open the individual PNGs.
- text_overflow message: "1 text(s) wider or taller than their box and cut at the edge: "$287 left" (clipped)": it does not say which box limited it or by how much. It was also emitted in check_component when the cause was simply my test height (90), which it could have said ("container height 90 < content 104").
- check_component sweep cells are rendered at about half the requested width (340 asked, cells ~170), so the name ellipsizes ("Dinin...") in every cell. That made the sweep look worse than the real thing.
- build_kit's `gallery` for ProgressBar points to "CMP-06 Tabs and page dots", which is unrelated.
- build_kit `icons.suggestions` gave "overview" for "overview", which is useless. No icons were suggested for the categories (the obvious need).
- inspect accessibility does not say whether a container's contentDescription replaces or merges with its children, so I cannot tell what TalkBack actually reads (redundant "Groceries" + "$312 / $400" after the label?).
- check_component reports the pattern mode as "expanded_because: the body calls another component", which is fine. It does not suggest how much the 14 ProgressBars cost (252 float expressions); I only found that with inspect bytes.

## 4. What I would have liked

- A semantics option to merge or hide descendants (one announcement per category row), or guidance on it.
- A way to pick a text from a condition inside a contentDescription (a "left"/"over" word), without the canvas-global textSubtext trick.
- The render sample cap stated, and a warning when it truncates.
- A non-overlapping contact sheet, or a separate sheet per size class.
- build_kit icon suggestions for category nouns in the request (groceries, rent, transport...). I guessed the names (shopping_cart, home, restaurant, directions_car, movie, bolt, warning) and they all existed, which was luck.
- A cheaper ProgressBar mode for a static-density widget (the mini bars don't need animation).

## 5. Assessment

- **Correctness**: the summary shows total spent (the sum of the 6 host floats), "of $budget", an overshoot bar, and "$X left" / a warning icon with "$X over budget". Each category shows an icon, the host-string name (ellipsized), "$spent / $limit" (grouped, whole dollars), and a ProgressBar with overshoot (a limit tick and a red excess). Below it is "$X left", or a warning icon with "Over by $X" in bold error colour. Checked: all under, one far over (Dining 620/150, total over budget), a zero limit with spend (a full red bar, "Over by $35"), zero/zero ("$0 / $0", "$0 left", a dot), exactly at the limit ("$0 left", not over), long names (ellipsis), and amounts up to $250,000.
- **Host variables**: name1..6 (string), spent1..6, limit1..6, budget (floats). Totals are computed in the document. The fixed number of categories (6) is a limit: the app cannot send 5 or 8.
- **Sizes**: a stateLayout on `step(windowHeight(), 260)` switches between a widget layout (total / budget / status, summary bar, and a 3x2 grid of mini cells with an icon, name, an over-warning icon and a thin overshoot bar) and the full scrolling phone list. Narrow, short and small sizes are fine. On a tablet it is a stretched single column with empty space below: functional but not tablet-designed.
- **Themes / mono**: theme colours only. Light, dark, mono-dark and mono-light were all checked. In mono, "over" is readable through the warning icon, the "Over by" text and the hatched excess. In mono-light the summary card loses its surface fill, so the card has no outline (it would need a mono ring, which is only reachable through a component `$if`).
- **Accessibility**: header description; each category column is labelled "Name, spent $X of $Y", and its status text is announced as "Over limit by $Z" or "Within limit, $Z left"; the summary is labelled "Spent $X of $Y budget"; the widget's over icons carry the label "over limit". Weak spot: the child texts are probably read as well (redundant), and I could not verify merging.
- **Bytes**: 29.9 KB, which is heavy. About 7 KB is ProgressBar expressions (14 bars), 4.4 KB is icons, and the widget plus phone layouts are both carried.
- **Audit**: 0 findings; render findings: none in the final variants.
