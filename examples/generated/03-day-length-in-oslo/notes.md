## 2. Dead ends and wasted calls

- **Calls 9–10 (2 calls):** my own JSON syntax error. The tools surfaced it as a raw Node stack trace (`convert`) and nine identical one-liners (`render` variants) rather than a position with context.
- **Call 15 (1 call):** `find` for a `flow` layout returned nothing about flow; the catalog's `layout.flow` capability matched but no example was returned. I never found out the flow syntax.
- **Call 16 (1 call):** `check_component`'s `call` parameter takes the bare params, not `{"Duration": {...}}`; the tool description says "call" and the component's own `call` field in `get` output *is* the wrapped form, so I copied that shape.
- **Calls 22–25 (4 calls):** zsh word splitting in my loop; not the server's fault.
- **Step semantics (one render cycle + 1 inspect):** `reference(expressions)` documents `step(edge, x)`; the engine implements `step(a, b) = a > b`. The example TIM-13 uses it the implemented way, so the two sources disagree and the reference loses.
- **fitBox in C++ (calls 21, 26–29, 33–38: 12 calls):** half the session went into finding out how the C++ fitBox decides what "fits" (natural widths, hidden texts included) and why it then mis-sizes the chosen child. Neither behaviour is documented in LAY-09's pitfalls, and the TS inspector disagrees with the C++ renderer, so each tool told me a different story.

## 3. Wrong, misleading, noisy or missing

- **Wrong:** `reference(expressions)` says `step(edge, x)`. The compiled formula (inspect) is `(a > b)`; i.e. `step(x, edge)`. TIM-13's `step(@now, @t0)` only works because of that. This silently flipped "longer/shorter than yesterday".
- **Misleading:** LineChart's `fill: true` pitfall ("the plot takes what the labels leave") — in the C++ player inside a fitBox the label row gets 0 px. Fixed `height` behaves the same there, so the real culprit is the C++ fitBox, but nothing in LAY-09 or the fitBox guidance says the C++ and TS engines differ, or that the fit test is "sum of children's natural widths" rather than the laid-out width.
- **Misleading:** the `render` size warning says "at 270×330 the content no longer fits" while the narrow variant it rendered is 259 wide; the number is not the variant's size.
- **Inconsistent output:** in the variants result some blocks (wide, short) print only "[image saved]" with no png_path/Success/findings line, while others print all three; I could not tell whether "no findings" or "findings not printed".
- **Noisy:** `needs_component: ["across"]` with a full component-writing recipe, because a preposition didn't match the vocabulary. `unmatched_terms` already said it; the recipe was 400 words of irrelevant guidance.
- **Noisy:** `build_kit` returned the entire TIM-12 solar document (2.6 KB canvas snippet, the whole TimeTile component, the header row) — 45 KB total — when all I needed was "declination from dayOfYear, acos of a clamped argument".
- **Noisy:** the component_overflow warning lists 13 identical texts, four of them spelled out in full, on every variant.
- **Noisy:** `check_component` warns "no summary" and "no call example" for an inline component that will never be in the library.
- **Missing:** `xLabels` are centred under evenly spaced x values, so there is no way to label 12 month *spans*; I used 13 boundary letters ("J … D J"). A `xLabelsAt: "boundaries" | "centres"` or an explicit x-per-label list would have been better.
- **Missing:** no way to see the C++ layout tree; `inspect` is the TS engine, `render` is C++, and when they disagree the only C++ evidence is the warning text.
- **Missing:** the audit's `hardcoded_date` info cannot be told "this date is a fixed astronomical event"; harmless here but there is no way to acknowledge it.
- **Missing:** `convert` crashing with a Node stack trace on invalid JSON instead of a structured error.
- **Not wrong but costly:** the 20-entry `fn` limit forced me to approximate the refraction term (`@k2` constant instead of `/ cos δ`), about ±1 minute at Oslo. The limit is documented; a pointer to "precompute what you can into variables" is there too, so this is fine, just tight.

## 4. What I would have liked

- A `step` entry in the reference that matches the engine (or the engine fixed), and a lint in `convert`/`audit` for `step(literal, expr)` patterns that look like GLSL order.
- A note in the fitBox guidance (LAY-09 pitfalls, `format` reference) that the C++ fit test is width-and-height on *natural* sizes, counts hidden texts, and ignores weights/ellipsis; and a mention that the two engines can disagree.
- `render` to print the same three lines (png_path, Success, findings) for every variant, and to name the actual variant size in the size warning.
- A compact `build_kit` mode: for a chart over a function I needed LineChart's params and three sentences of TIM-12, not the whole document.
- `check_component` error message to show the expected `call` shape, and `get <component>`'s `call` field to be in the same shape `check_component` accepts.
- A short "C++ vs TS" diagnostic: when a render finding is zero_size/component_overflow and `inspect layout` at the same size is clean, say so (or offer `inspect` through the C++ engine).
- `LineChart`: an option to put xLabels at interval centres vs boundaries; and `yLabels` auto from y_min/y_max/step.

## 5. Assessment of the final widget

**Correctness.** Day length = 7.6394·acos(k₂ − tan φ · tan δ), δ = 0.40928·sin(0.0172·(d − 81)), with k₂ = sin(−0.833°)/cos φ taken at the mean cos δ. Checked against known values: Oslo 30 Oct → 8 h 39 min shown (NOAA ≈ 8 h 40 min); 14 Dec → 5 h 56 min (≈ 5 h 57 min); longest 18 h 48 min (actual 18 h 50 min); shortest 5 h 52 min (actual 5 h 54 min). Within 2–3 minutes all year; the 20-entry limit is the only reason it is not within one minute. Today's marker is `now: "dayOfYear()"`, so it moves by itself (the 45-days-later render shows it at mid-December at 5 h 56 min). The "N min less/more than yesterday" line is computed from the same formula at d − 1 and its sign was verified (October → less; the earlier inversion came from the step() order). Latitude and place are exported host variables, so the playground can move it to another city (only the title and the "Jun 21 / Dec 21" labels would be wrong south of the equator).

**Layout at small sizes.** fitBox with three alternatives: full (318 high: header, date, today, change line, chart with 5 y-labels, longest/shortest tiles) → compact (152 high: title, today, chart with 3 y-labels) → minimal (52 high: title and number). In the TS engine (the published page) it switches cleanly and nothing is clipped from 259 wide up. In the C++ renders the compact chart column is mis-sized (plot 80 instead of 84, label row 0 px) but the labels and marker are still drawn where they belong, so the picture is right even though the findings say otherwise. Wide-and-short (576×263) shows compact with ~90 px empty below; a medium alternative would fill it.

**Light/dark.** Both use theme roles only (`@colors.primary` line with a 16 % wash, muted labels, warning-coloured sun icon); light render is as legible as dark.

**Mono / mono-inverted.** The chart drops the wash and draws a 2 dp onSurface line with a grey grid and now-line, white-on-black and black-on-white both fine; the sun icon and all text go monochrome. No alpha fills remain.

**Weak points.** The 13 month letters are at month boundaries, not centred on months (LineChart has no span labels); the "J" at both ends is a hint of that. "Jun 21 / Dec 21" are literal (solstice dates; the audit flags them as info). No interaction. The compact alternative leaves slack at wide sizes.
