# Eval 54, run 2: flight status card (Paris to Singapore)

Final document: `flight.json` (generated by `make.py`), converted to `flight.rc` (22,362 B).
Renders are in `out/`. The contact sheets are `out/sheet-samples2.png` (5 flight states, light), `out/sheet-widget.png` (4 looks, widget), `out/sheet-widget2.png` and `out/sheet-widget3.png` (widget sizes).

## 1. Tool calls (19 in total: 1 list + 18 calls)
1. `list`: to learn the servers and the workflow. It said to start with build_kit and gave the steps (build_kit, convert check, render, audit). I followed that.
2. `build_kit` (the goal verbatim): to get components and examples. It returned WorldMap, which has `routeFrom`/`routeTo` as a great circle, `progress` and a plane icon turned to its heading. That is exactly the map part. It also gave MAP-03 (a hand-built flight map), Card/ListItem, and design rules. A design rule even quotes `"routeFrom": "Paris", "routeTo": "Singapore", "progress": "@p"`. I decided to use WorldMap and skip the hand-built canvas.
3. `reference` query "time zone utc offset epoch seconds": to learn how to handle time zones. Only the device's `offsetToUtc()` exists, with no tz database. It gave the epoch-day formula, the 24-bit float warning, and said epochSecond() is rejected by Android. I decided the instants come from the app as UTC epoch day + UTC minute of day, and each airport's UTC offset (minutes) comes from the app too.
4. `get MAP-03`: to see how "h left" is formatted (textMerge + textFromFloat, flags 5/4). I copied the formatting pattern.
5. `find components` "status chip TextChoice ...": for the status word and the progress bar. I used TextChoice (status, weekday, phase) and ProgressBar. Chip is a toggle, so it was not used.
6. `convert` check: the first build. It caught `padding {left,right}` being ignored, and "In flight" was clipped. I fixed the padding keys.
7. `render` variants (clock pinned, offset 3600): the first look. The status pill wrapped "In flight". The compact layout under 300 tall had wrapped captions and "UTC…" cut off. I fixed the pill (width 96, maxLines 1) and moved the city into the caption.
8. `render` samples + clocks: I wanted 4 states, each at its own clock. The clocks were silently ignored, and every sample used the call time ("12 h 03 min" everywhere). This was a partial dead end.
9. `reference` query "render samples clocks together": to find out how to combine them. No answer. Dead end.
10. `render` samples (5 states, one pinned clock; I moved the flight instants instead of the clock): before departure, 10 %, 50 %, landed, delayed 145 min. I hand-checked every time and countdown and all were correct.
11. `render` 360x180: to check the compact (widget) layout. It was good.
12. `audit`: found 2 unused expressions (dayDiff/hasDayDiff, from an arrival "+1" I never displayed). I removed them.
13. `inspect accessibility`: to see what a screen reader gets. It reads in a sensible order. The map has its label. The weekday and UTC offset are separate fragments.
14. `inspect summary`: bytes, repaint, host variables. Repaint is "scheduled" (about once a minute) and the audit is clean.
15. `render` variants at 360x180: all widget sizes. 4x2 at 320x160 was fine. Short (108), 4x1 (80), 2x2 (160x160) and tiny were zero-size or clipped. I added a minimal layout for height < 140.
16. `render` variants again: the short sizes were fine. 2x2 and tiny (narrow) were still clipped. I added a narrow layout for width < 240.
17. `render` variants again: every size was clean, with no findings.
18. `audit` final: 0 errors, 0 warnings. Info: repeated_panes (11 texts across 4 stateLayout panes), the longest expression at 30 of 32 entries, and deep nesting.
19. `convert` final: wrote flight.rc, 22,362 B.

## 2. Dead ends and wasted calls (2)
- Call 8: `samples` together with `clocks` ignores `clocks` without saying so. A one-line notice ("clocks ignored when samples given"), or support for `clock` inside each sample, would have avoided it.
- Call 9: the reference query for the same question returned unrelated paragraphs.
- Partly wasted: calls 15 and 16 were iterations on small widget sizes. A build_kit rule saying "for a 4x2 widget, plan a stateLayout for under ~140 tall and under ~240 wide" would have saved one.

## 3. Wrong, misleading, noisy or missing in tool output
- render: "clocks: [...] (several pinned dates in one call)" and "samples: [...]" are documented side by side, but combined, the clocks are silently dropped (call 8 output: "sample 1: progress=0 status=0 → ... findings: none", with no mention of the clock).
- render variants "seven hours later" (call 7) showed "4 h 50 min" where 10 h 50 − 7 h = 3 h 50 was expected. It looks like the variant does not keep `system_variables {"10": 3600}` or the pinned clock, or it shifts something else. The output does not say which clock or offset that variant used.
- build_kit `resolved` mapped "progress" to `use_case:loading-state` and "local time" to `expr.system` (EXP-13, a canvas diagnostics demo). That is noise for this goal. Time zones were not resolved at all, and `not_covered: []` claims full coverage while local time in another city's zone is not supported.
- No mention anywhere of time zones other than the device's (`offsetToUtc()`). That is the key constraint of "each local time", and I only learned it by inference from the reference.
- The design rule "Rows from the app" says minutes until a time come from `timeInMin()` with a midnight wrap. That is wrong for a flight crossing days and time zones: you need day + minute instants in UTC. The epoch-day recipe further down the reference is the right one, but it is only for dates.
- The convert warning for padding was helpful: "padding {left, right} is ignored. ... Write {"padding": {"start": 4}}".
- WorldMap `label` is a static string, so the map's accessible text cannot carry the progress.
- The text_overflow message for the status pill named the text but not the cause (TextChoice inside a box, in a row with a weighted sibling, wrapping to two lines).

## 4. What I would have liked
- A time-zone story: either a documented host-variable convention (offset per place) in build_kit for "local time in X", or a component that formats an instant in a given offset.
- A `Countdown` / duration-format helper ("h min" from minutes, phrased as before / after / done).
- `samples` entries that accept `clock` (or a samples × clocks matrix).
- A dynamic accessibility label for WorldMap (e.g. "42 % of the way").
- A ready "flight status" example combining WorldMap with times. MAP-03 is a lower-level canvas version.

## 5. Honest assessment
- **Correctness:** the route is WorldMap's great circle (49 samples projected to plate carrée), so it arcs north over Eastern Europe and Central Asia before bending down to Singapore. That is the shape of the real route on a flat map, though real flights deviate for airspace. The plane sits at `@progress` along the arc (by distance), turned to its heading. Times: the app sends UTC instants as `depDay/depMin`, `arrDay/arrMin` (epoch day + minute, exact in float), plus `depOff`/`arrOff` minutes (Paris 60 or 120 for DST, Singapore 480). Local hh:mm, weekday (from the epoch day) and "UTC+N" are computed in the document. The countdown (Departs in / Arrives in / Landed) is computed from the device clock converted to UTC with `offsetToUtc()`, so it works in any device zone. All 5 samples were hand-checked.
- **Host variables:** depDay, depMin, arrDay, arrMin, depOff, arrOff, progress, status (0 Scheduled, 1 Boarding, 2 In flight, 3 Delayed, 4 Landed, 5 Cancelled), delayMin. Progress comes from the app as asked; it is not derived from the clock.
- **Sizes:** stateLayout picks among 4 panes: the full phone card (360x440), compact (map between the times, at heights 140–300, which covers 4x2 at 320x160 and 360x180), minimal (under 140 tall: CDG time, bar, SIN time) and narrow (under 240 wide). Every variant size now renders without findings.
- **Themes and mono:** only theme colours are used. Light, dark, mono-dark and mono-light were all checked. In mono the map becomes an outline and is a bit busy at widget size.
- **Repaint:** scheduled, about once a minute (reads timeInMin).
- **Accessibility:** a document description, and the map labelled. All texts are real layout texts in reading order. The weekday and UTC offset are read as fragments ("Sat", " · UTC+1"). The progress bar has no value of its own; the "Arrives in" text carries it.
- **Bytes:** 22.4 KB. About 6.7 KB is map paths (two WorldMap instances, low detail) and about 6 KB is expressions. The audit's repeated_panes info is justified: time texts are repeated across the 4 panes.
- **Weak spots:**
  - There is no time-zone database: the app must send offsets, and DST is the app's job.
  - Negative UTC offsets would print "UTC+-5", which does not matter for this route.
  - The delay is shown as "Departure delayed by 145 min" and not as "2 h 25".
  - "Landed" removes the duration text, so the row's height shifts by about 2 px.
  - There is no "+1 day" marker; the weekday shows it instead.
  - The longest expression is at 30 of the 32 Android allows.
  - The status pill's width is fixed at 96, so a long translation would be cut.
  - "Flight 256" is a placeholder text, not a host string.
