# Prompt 33, run 2: Day / Week / Month segmented control

Final document: `activity.json` (it is generated by `build.py` from `periodbars.json`, my inline component), which converts to `activity.rc` (13,165 B).
I count 17 tool calls (`list` is not counted; it is the wrapper's catalogue). 2 of them were dead ends and 1 was wasted.

## 1. Tool calls in order
1. build_kit (goal in plain words) gave me Tabs (`style: "segmented"`, with `selected` and an `onClick` hostNamedAction carrying `@sel` and `valueType` float), BarChart, TIM-10 (days in the month and month-name formulas) and design rules. I chose Tabs for the control. I decided to write my own bar chart, because BarChart has no highlight, count or "stop at now" option.
2. get BarChart (definition: true) gave me the canvas-loop structure to copy.
3. reference expressions gave me arraySum/arrayGet, the calendar formulas, the 32-entry limit and step semantics.
4. reference format gave me visibility (it needs declared 0/1 variables), textMerge/textFromFloat flags, the canvas global block for named texts and semantics with a text object.
5. check_component PeriodBars (doc with a week array, sweep highlight 0/3/6). It returned ok with no warnings, and the renders were right in light, dark and mono.
6. convert check: it failed with `textTransform: missing required field 'start'`. (Dead end.)
7. reference query "textTransform trim start len" returned nothing about the field. (Wasted.) I guessed `start: 0, len: 9`.
8. convert check: ok, 10,583 B. The only remark was "unused_space" info.
9. render, light, 14:30. Day view is OK. I spotted that the day total included future hours, so I decided to mask the totals.
10. inspect interact `{"type":"tap","x":..}`: error, wrong action shape. (Dead end, but the error text gave the right shape.)
11. inspect interact `{"tap":[180,97]}` (tap Week): view 0→1, host_actions viewSelected=1, the Day panel hidden and the Week panel shown, and the Day tab's state flipped. Verified.
12. render samples view 0/1/2: all three views look right. The week total still counted Sunday (the future), so I masked the week and month totals to "so far".
13. render clocks 2026-10-31, 2027-02-28, 2027-02-10 with view=2: 31 bars, then 28 bars (wider), "February 2027", 82,300 = the sum of the first 10 values (checked by hand).
14. render variants (view=1): dark bars on `@colors.track` were barely visible, so I switched them to `@colors.muted`. At the short size it overflowed, so I added verticalScroll to the root (it is a phone screen).
15. audit: 0 errors and 0 warnings; the longest expression is 29 of 32 entries.
16. inspect accessibility (view=2): each tab is labelled with Selected / Not selected, and the panel reads "October 2026, 264,900 steps". Hidden panels are not exposed.
17. render variants again plus convert: no findings at any size; 13,165 B.

## 2. Dead ends and wasted calls
- Calls 6 and 7: textTransform. The format reference documents only `operation` ("textTransform takes "operation" as a NUMBER") and never says that `start`/`len` are required or what they mean. A query for exactly those words returned unrelated paragraphs. A complete field list in the reference, or an example snippet in build_kit's TIM-10 entry (it shows only the textSubtext half), would have avoided both calls.
- Call 10: interact's `actions` shape is not in the `list` description. I guessed `{"type":"tap"}`. Its error message was good. I had not run `describe inspect`, which would have avoided this.

## 3. Wrong, misleading, noisy or missing output
- TIM-10's teaching says "then textTransform operation 3 (trim)", but none of its snippets shows the textTransform JSON.
- BarChart has no way to mark today or now, and draws every value in the array. build_kit's `needs_component` was absent, although the goal said "today highlighted" and "days of the current month". The design rule "Today's curve stops at now … LineChart's upTo" exists for lines only.
- There is no partial-sum function (arraySum over a range). Getting "total so far" needed 3-term generated variables (8 for the day, 3 for the week, 11 for the month) to stay under 32 entries. Nothing in the reference suggests a better way.
- Noisy: `unused_space` info on a phone screen with a scrolling layout ("add what the screen is for (history, details, actions)").
- Missing: no contrast check. Non-highlighted bars in `@colors.track` on `@colors.surface` in dark were nearly invisible, and render reported "findings: none".
- The Tabs pitfall "valueType is a json2rc extension: the Java JSON parser does not write the value" means the action carries no index with the Java parser. The kit offers no portable alternative, such as one action name per tab.
- inspect accessibility lists two identical "Day" / "Week" / "Month" text nodes per tab (the cross-fade copies). It does not say whether a reader announces them twice.
- check_component's sweep renders light only. I had to look at mono separately.

## 4. What I would have liked
- A BarChart with `highlight`, `count` and `upTo`, or `needs_component` saying so.
- `arraySum(array, from, to)` or a documented prefix-sum recipe.
- Full field lists for each text command (textSubtext/textTransform) in reference format.
- A contrast finding in render.
- A note on whether the duplicated tab texts are read twice.

## 5. Assessment of the final screen
- **Structure:** a title "Activity", then the Tabs segmented control (Day/Week/Month), then a surface card with three stacked panels switched by visibility. Each panel shows the period label, the big total with "steps", a caption, and the PeriodBars chart (200 dp).
- **Correctness:**
  - Day has 24 hourly bars with ticks at 00/06/12/18. The current hour is highlighted with a dot under it, later hours are not drawn, and the total covers hours so far.
  - Week has M…S (Monday start), today highlighted, and future days not drawn.
  - Month has nd bars from the calendar (28 to 31, leap rule), ticks at 1/8/15/22 and today highlighted.
  - Labels are "Today", "This week" and month name + year from the clock.
  - All of this was verified at pinned clocks and by a hand-checked sum.
- **Interactions:** tapping a tab sets the exported `view` and sends `hostNamedAction viewSelected` with the index (verified with inspect interact).
- **Host variables:** `view` (float, exported) and the float arrays `day` (24), `week` (7) and `month` (31), all exported.
- **Sizes:** no findings from narrow 259 to tablet 792. Short sizes scroll. On a tablet the chart stretches wide; a max width would look better.
- **Themes and mono:** light and dark follow the theme. In mono, the bars are outlined and the highlight bar is solid. The selected tab is a solid pill with inverted text, so it is readable in mono-dark and mono-light.
- **Accessibility:** tabs announce label + Selected/Not selected. The panel announces "<period>, <total> steps". The chart is labelled. Child texts are also readable, so some is repeated.
- **Bytes:** 13,165 B. A large part is the masked-sum variables and the Tabs' text ops.
- **Known weak spots:**
  - The week starts on Monday (not locale-aware) and the hour ticks use the 24 h format.
  - The month array must have 31 entries; if it is shorter, arrayGet goes out of range.
  - Host values for future slots are ignored on purpose.
  - The action index relies on the json2rc-only `valueType`.
  - timeInHr() makes it repaint once a minute (not checked with inspect runtime).
  - The highlighted bar uses only colour and fill, plus the dot.
