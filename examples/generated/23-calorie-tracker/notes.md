# Calorie tracker widget: eval notes (prompt 23, run 1)

Deliverables: `doc.json` (source, generated by `gen.py`), `doc.rc` (11,920 B), `calorie-tracker.html` (playground),
inline components `ring_comp.json` / `bar_comp.json` (the MacroRow and MacroMini components are written in `gen.py`), renders in `out/`
(contact sheets: `out/sheet_v2.png` for the variants, `out/sheet_samples2.png` for the sample values, `out/sheet_states.png` for mono / 2x2 / 4x1 over target).

## 1. Tool calls (27 in total)

1. `list`: workflow and tool list. Decided to follow the workflow: build_kit, then inline components plus check_component, then convert, render and audit.
2. `build_kit` (the user's goal, verbatim): it proposed BarChart, ProgressRing and Icon, plus the examples A11Y-04, TIM-14 and STR-09. "macro" was taken to mean a *macro* (STR-09, modifier macros), and "calorie/protein/carb" were unmatched. Kept ProgressRing as a starting point and dropped BarChart: it is a column chart scaled to the max of the array, so it can't show each macro against its own target. Noted the design rule that mentions ProgressBar.
3. `get ProgressBar`: it has no over-target state, and "The fill is at least a round dot ... so 0 % is not empty". I decided to write my own bar.
4. `get ProgressRing definition:true`: "Not clamped: keep it in range", so an overshoot just paints a full ring. Copied its structure (unit box, s/translate/scale, animated local) for my own CalorieRing with a second lap in `@colors.error` past the target.
5. `reference format`: very useful. Gave me the widget sizes (4x2 320×160, 4x1 320×80, 2x2 160×160), the fitBox rules, visibility needing a declared 0/1 variable, the textFromFloat flags (21 = grouped), and semantics with a textMerge description.
6. `reference components`: inline component syntax (locals, `$if theme.mono`, the alpha carry-over trap) and the mono rules ("Show state by shape, not colour"). That led to the mono overflow drawn as a thinner inner lap and outlined bar segments.
7. `reference expressions`: confirmed `step(x, edge)` = 1 when x > edge (value first) and `clamp(min,max,x)`.
8. `describe check_component`: needed it for the sweep's shape.
9. `check_component CalorieRing` with a sweep of 0 / 0.5 / 1 / 1.3 / 2.2 in light and mono: correct in every cell. `columns: 5` was ignored (the sheet came out with 2 columns).
10. `check_component MacroBar` with a sweep of eaten 0 / 60 / 120 / 156 / 300: found a bug in my drawing (a red or outlined sliver at the right end when not over, caused by a negative-width rect). Fixed it by moving the over segment off the canvas when not over.
11. `convert check:true`: ok, 11,891 B, 8 host variables listed. Got a `[text_only]` suggestion, which is false: there are 13 canvases with rings and bars.
12. `describe render`: to learn `variants`, `samples` and `componentTheme`.
13. `render variants` (v1): the tiny size (160×96) showed nothing (fitbox_no_fit), and "C 160g" was clipped at 4x1. I shortened the compact texts ("1,450 kcal" without the target) so the last fitBox child fits 136 wide.
14. `render variants` (v2): every variant had "findings: none". Looked at the contact sheet: all good.
15. `render samples` (0, half, at target, 30 % over, one macro over, and an extreme case with target 0 and 12345 g): the "+20 over" label was glued to the macro name, and `whole: 4` printed 12345 as "2345". Fixed the row (spacer with a weight, over text next to the value) and set whole to 5.
16. `inspect accessibility` (default size): nodes with absurd bounds (w 1.89, h 0.82) and the compact layout B instead of the wide one. Dead end, see 2.
17. `inspect accessibility` at 320×160: still layout B with the same absurd bounds.
18. `inspect layout` at 320×160: explained it. In the TS engine the wide row "needs 296.24×130" in a 296 box (0.24 dp over), so the fitBox fell back to B, while the C++ render showed the wide layout. Shrank the ring from 130 to 122 and the gap from 16 to 14.
19. `inspect accessibility` at 320×160: wide layout. Calories, "600 over" and each macro are announced ("Protein, 140 of 120 grams"). The inspector puts "+20 over" (x 252, w 46) on top of "140 / 120 g" (x 254). The C++ renders don't overlap.
20. `render samples` (light; 0 / at target / 30 % over / one macro over): all readable.
21. `render componentTheme mono` with over samples: the inner lap and outlined bar overflow are visible, and the "over" text carries the meaning.
22. `render 160×160` (2x2) with over and zero: good.
23. `render 320×80` (4x1) with over and at target: good.
24. `audit`: 0 errors, 0 warnings, plus the info `text_only` (false again) and fitbox_shows child 1.
25. `convert check:true` (final): 11,920 B.
26. `inspect bytes`: 33 % expressions, 24 % text. There are 4 fitBox layouts, so roughly 4× the texts.
27. `publish` with variable_ranges → `calorie-tracker.html` (708 KB page). It still opens with "NOT DONE YET" plus the size warning, and publishes anyway.

## 2. Dead ends and wasted calls

- Calls 16–17 (accessibility at the default size, then repeated at 320×160): the bounds were nonsense and the layout was wrong, and nothing said why. Only `inspect layout` (call 18) showed the 0.24 dp fitBox miss. If the accessibility output had included the fitBox diagnostic (which child is shown and why), I would have saved a call. And if the C++ render and the TS inspector agreed on text widths, I would have saved the whole detour.
- Calls 8 and 12 (describe): needed only because `list` truncates descriptions. Not really waste, but the sweep and samples shapes could be in the short description.
- Total: 2 dead-end calls (16, 17).

## 3. Wrong, misleading, noisy or missing

- **False `text_only`** on every convert, render, audit and publish: "A screen of 13 texts and nothing else. No icon, drawing, chart or image". The document has 13 canvases (a ring and bars). It is presented with "NOT DONE YET: this document has design problems to fix", so a new user would think the widget is broken.
- **Persistent size warning** on render variants and publish: "at 230×160 the content no longer fits (cut off or squeezed to nothing)". The narrow render at 230×160 shows the compact layout intact, with "findings: none". This looks like it is triggered by the fitBox's first (hidden) child. It contradicts the per-image findings.
- **build_kit misread "macros"**: it resolved them to `reuse.macro`/`struct.macro` and offered STR-09 (modifier macros), which is irrelevant. "protein", "carb" and "calorie" were unmatched. A fitness or nutrition example (or a "progress against a target, with overshoot" pattern) would have helped. A11Y-04 (touch targets) was included "about: the goal's subject: target, fat", a keyword hit on "target"/"fat".
- **build_kit suggested BarChart** for "macros as bars". It is a column chart normalised to the array's max, so it can't show each value against its own target. ProgressBar (more fitting) appears only in prose under the design rules, not in `components`.
- **ProgressRing/ProgressBar have no overshoot**: "Not clamped: keep it in range". There is no guidance for the very common "over target" case. Also, ProgressBar's "0 % is not empty" (a minimum dot) is wrong for a tracker.
- **TS inspector and C++ renderer disagree on layout**: text widths differ enough to flip a fitBox (296.24 vs 296), and overlapping text bounds were reported where the render shows none. The accessibility task returned bounds like `w 1.89, h 0.82` with no warning.
- `check_component` sweep `columns: 5` was ignored.
- `textFromFloat whole` silently truncates the leading digits (12345 → "2345"). It is documented in the components guide ("whole: 1 prints 10 as 0"), but nothing flags it at render time.
- `inspect bytes` lists 16 `LoopOperation`s in a document with no loops. That is confusing (probably the converter's internals).

## 4. What I would have liked

- A library ProgressRing/ProgressBar with an `overflow` mode (a second lap or segment in error colour, and a mono variant), or a "goal tracker" example.
- build_kit domain synonyms: nutrition, macros (protein/carbs/fat), calories → fitness/progress-vs-target, not modifier macros.
- `text_only` that counts canvases and component drawings, and a size warning that respects fitBox fallbacks.
- One "check host samples × sizes × themes" call (samples combined with variants), instead of one render per size.
- The accessibility inspect showing which fitBox branch is active and flagging degenerate bounds.

## 5. Assessment of the final widget

- **Correctness**: the ring shows eaten/target. Past the target, a second lap in error red starts at 12 o'clock (in mono, a thin inner lap), and the centre switches from "N left" (primary) to "N over" (error, bold). Remaining and over are computed in the document (`max(0, target-kcal)`, `max(0, kcal-target)`, `isOver = step(kcal, target)`). Each macro bar is scaled to max(eaten, target): the part up to the target is in the macro colour and the excess in error red (outlined in mono), with "+N over" and "eaten / target g" texts. Verified at 0, half, exactly at target ("0 left", full ring), 30 % over (2,600/2,000 → "600 over", red lap) and one macro over (protein 150/120 → "+30 over").
- **Host variables** (exported floats): kcal 1450, kcalTarget 2000, protein 92/120, carbs 160/250, fat 48/65. A target of 0 is guarded with max(1, …).
- **Sizes**: a fitBox with 4 layouts. The wide one (ring 122 + three labelled bars) is used at 4x2 320×160, on the phone card and wider. The square one (ring + three mini bars) is used at 2x2 160×160 and at narrow 4x2 widths. The strip (small ring + kcal + mini bars) is used at 4x1 320×80 and short sizes. The minimal one (ring + kcal) is used for tiny sizes. Every variant renders with no findings.
- **Themes**: theme colours only. Light, dark, mono and mono-inverted were checked. In mono, "over" is shown by shape (inner lap, outlined segment) plus the word "over".
- **Accessibility**: header "Calorie tracker". The ring box is "Calories 2,600 of 2,000 kcal", and the "600 over"/"550 left" text is read. Each macro reads "Protein, 140 of 120 grams" (and "+20 over" as text in the wide layout).
- **Bytes**: 11,920 B .rc. That is heavy, because 4 layouts repeat the texts and each bar/ring is expanded (about 370 B per bar instance, 13 canvases).
- **Weak spots**:
  - The wide layout is only 22 dp wider than the space it needs, and the TS and C++ engines measure text differently, so a long "+NNNN over" can push one engine to the square layout.
  - The compact layouts show grams eaten without targets ("P 92g"), and the 4x1/2x2 drop "of 2,000 kcal" (the left/over figure implies it).
  - The macro over state isn't in the compact layouts' text, only in the bar colour or outline.
  - "0 left" at exactly the target could read "Goal reached".
  - The overlapping text bounds reported by the TS inspector were not reproduced in the renders but are unexplained.
